Nov 29 – Dec 5, 2026 · 7 Days · Kruger National Park

South Africa Yoga Safari Retreat

A transformational yoga safari experience designed to help guests reset, revive, and feel fully alive. Set over 7 days in the heart of Kruger National Park, this immersive journey blends daily yoga, breathwork, meditation, and nature-based healing with the raw beauty and power of the African wilderness.

Each day is intentionally structured to support nervous system reset, emotional restoration, and deep reconnection to self through embodied practices and time in nature. This retreat offers a rare opportunity to step out of routine life and into a deeply present state of awareness, connection, and vitality.

2027 · Limited to 12 spaces

Portugal Retreat

Experience a profound nervous system reset during this intimate four-day retreat on the sacred land of Shaman João in Portugal. Through powerful gong and drum healing, breathwork, cacao ceremony, beach excursions, nature immersion, and sacred drum circles, you'll be invited to slow down, reconnect, and restore.

A highlight of the retreat is crafting your own shamanic drum by hand and participating in a unique overnight sound healing journey featuring nearly eight hours of continuous healing vibrations. With a small group setting and the beauty of Portugal surrounding us, this retreat offers a deeply restorative experience for the mind, body, and spirit.
